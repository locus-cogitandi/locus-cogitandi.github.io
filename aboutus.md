## Locus Cogitandi

A few years ago, this project started as a roundtable discussion of Latin writing during lunch breaks. The idea was simple. Create a place for thinking. Eventually, this led to writing some blog posts. Recently, we’ve decided to expand this into **a literary journal at the intersection of philosophy, classical texts, and the present-day experience run by students at the Cambridge School**.

## Our Team

## Beacons of Advise

The following are a list of people who have signifcantly influenced our thought process & taste and/or who we occasionally go to for guidance on reading & selection. 

Teacher (Latin & Greek) / Advisor, **Jim Hamilton** studied at Westminster Seminary California and UC Irvine; he is the language chair at the Cambridge School in San Diego, where he teaches Latin and Ancient Greek. 

Teacher (Writing) / Advisor, **Doug Jones** has had a wide range of literary experiences. He received an M.A. in philosophy from the University of Southern California and an M.F.A. in creative writing from the University of Idaho. He went on to teach philosophy and literature at the University of Idaho, Lewis-Clark State College, and New Saint Andrews College (where he was also a member of the Board of Trustees). He has also served as editorial director at Canon Press and senior editor of the Credenda/Agenda magazine. Throughout his life, he has come to a deep understanding of diversity from living in South Africa with Greek parents to teaching at a Muslim women’s college in the Middle East. He has written several books, including A Rhetoric of Love, Angels in the Architecture, Dismissing Jesus: How We Evade the Way of the Cross, as well as several children’s books, such as Huguenot Garden and Scottish Seas. He has also helped oversee Christian ministry in Myanmar.
